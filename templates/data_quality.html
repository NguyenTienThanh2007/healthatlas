WHY IT MATTERS

Quality-aware analytics

Every explorer filters unusable values before calculations. This page makes those exclusions visible instead of hiding them.

01Transparent exclusions

Missing or invalid values are surfaced, not silently treated as zero.

02Context for outliers

Coverage above 100% is flagged for interpretation rather than automatically deleted.

03Population validity

Population-based rates only use positive, available population records.
